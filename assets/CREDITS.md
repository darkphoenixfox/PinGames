# Asset credits & licenses

Every asset added to the repo must have a row here (D-001, D-020, D-032).

**License policy (D-032), because PinGames may be sold:**
- ✅ **CC0**, **CC BY** (preferred)
- ⚠️ **CC BY-SA**, **SIL OFL** (fonts): only with the director's approval
- ❌ Anything **-NC** (NonCommercial) or **-ND** (NoDerivatives), "personal use only", or no clear license
- AI tools (Meshy, music generators…): the plan used must grant **commercial rights**. Record the plan.

Keep proof of the license, such as a screenshot or saved page dated at download, in `assets/licenses/`.

**Attribution (TASL):** "Title" by Author (source URL), licensed under LICENSE (license URL); changes made.
